## Not included

- **Boj na salašu (as a standalone Wikisource poem)**: Wikisource lists it as attributed to Njegoš but probably not his. (The song also appears in Ogledalo srpsko, where it is included as part of the anthology.)
- **Glas kamenštaka**: Only a modern Serbian back-translation of the Italian text survives (ppnjegos.com vol. 6, njegos.org), not Njegoš's own wording. Excluded.
- **Prevodi (ppnjegos.com vol. 8)**: Njegoš's translations (e.g. from the Iliad via Russian). His wording but not his work; excluded. Easy to add if wanted.
- **Bilježnica (ppnjegos.com vol. 10)**: Personal notebook: mostly French/Russian/Italian excerpts, addresses, tables and numbers. Too noisy for a char-level model; excluded.
